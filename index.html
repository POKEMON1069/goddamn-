02 About

A designer who ships, and a developer who sweats the grid.

I'm Aayushman. I started on the design side — type, layout, the stubborn business of making a page feel inevitable — and taught myself enough code to stop handing drawings to other people. Now I do both, which means fewer translations and a lot less lost in between.

I work independently with founders and small teams, mostly on products that need a spine: a real type scale, a grid that means something, motion that tells you where you are. I care about the interval between the sketch and the commit being as short as possible.

Outside client work I build generative type and pixel experiments, and I'm slowly teaching myself the parts of graphics programming I don't know yet.

  • Design that survives productionIf it can't be built at 60fps on a mid-range phone, it isn't finished.
  • Performance is a design decisionWeight, loading order and font strategy belong in the design file, not the post-mortem.
  • Type firstGet the scale, rhythm and tracking right and half the layout solves itself.
